elixir mobile

A six voice drum machine for your phone, built on the LXR-02 engine. Press play, then start pressing the randomise buttons. Everything below is optional.

The basics

  • play starts and stops the beat.
  • The buttons along the bottom switch pages. The page you're on is named next to the ELIXIR title.
  • Drag any fader up or down to change it.
  • undo on the PERF page takes back the last randomise.

perf

Twelve faders for playing live, plus the randomise buttons.

  • everything makes a whole new beat: a new genre, groove, kit, effects and mix.
  • kit changes the sounds and pattern changes the beat. alter nudges the current sounds by about 10%.
  • crossfeed randomises how the voices modulate each other.
  • random fx sets new effects and sends. alter fx nudges them. Echo times stay musical.
  • Tick next bar to make every randomise land on the start of the next bar.
  • Tick assign, then tap a fader to choose what it controls.

beat

Tempo, genre, beat and energy, plus fills, the rush (a roll that builds on one drum while the others fall away), velocity, groove, depth and accent.

voice

Every setting for one drum. Choose the drum along the top and the section (oscillator, click, filter and so on) below it. The CLICK wave fader picks the attack sample: the 14 built in ones, then the extra clicks.

x/y

Two pads. Use the menus above each pad to choose what left-right and up-down control, then drag your finger around.

fx, mix and mstr

  • fx sets up the echo (tick ping pong to bounce it left and right) and the ring mod, with the crossfeed matrix under them: each time a drum (a row) hits, it pushes a setting on the drums it feeds (the columns: tap a column's box to pick pitch, decay, fm, drive, dark (the filter closes) or duck (the drum gets quieter)), which glides back over the glide time. Drag a cell up or down to set how hard, double tap it to clear.
  • mix has each drum's level and how much it sends to the two effects.
  • mstr is the master chain: the drum 1 sidechain duck, then distortion, then the compressor.

prst (presets)

  • Tap a preset to load it. It flashes until the next bar, then takes over.
  • Tap the star to make it a favourite.
  • save new stores what you hear. save over replaces the loaded preset.
  • Untick the tempo box to keep your tempo when loading.
  • export favourites saves your starred presets to a file. import reads one back.

Step sequencers

Hold any fader for half a second to open a step sequencer for it. Set the number of steps (2 to 16), the note length, depth and slew, or randomise the steps. The steps move the fader up and down from wherever you leave it, so you can still play it.

Recording

  • rec starts recording. Press it again to stop, and the WAV downloads.
  • loop records an exact, loopable WAV of a set number of bars and downloads it. Hold it to choose 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars.

Hidden extras

  • Hold a randomise button to open its settings: the ranges and chances it uses, with a tab for each randomise button.
  • The gear beside the title opens settings. Its RUSH tab is a graph of how the rush changes as it rises: the roll's speed, the other drums dropping out, the kick and the high-pass. Drag a square to move that break point.
  • MIDI CC 13 to 24 move the twelve PERF faders (nothing else answers MIDI), in Chrome with a MIDI controller plugged in.
  • To install it as an app, use install app on the presets page, or your browser's "Add to home screen".
